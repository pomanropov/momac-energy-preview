Counterparty onboarding

Before a trade moves forward we check who we are dealing with. We ask for identity and ownership information, screen the parties against applicable sanctions lists and collect the supporting documents the transaction needs.

Roles agreed before a trade

Each party's role is agreed before the trade starts. That covers who holds title, who carries delivery obligations and how payment is structured. These points are written into the agreements for the transaction.
