[03] Process

From requirement to delivery.

The commercial agreement and the physical movement have to work together. The critical requirements come into view early.

  1. Step 01

    Define the product.

    Confirm the specification, volume, supply availability, loading point, destination and delivery schedule.

    SpecificationVolumeLoad portSchedule
  2. Step 02

    Understand the counterparties.

    Establish each party's role, commercial requirements and supporting information before the trade moves forward.

    RolesRequirementsDocuments
  3. Step 03

    Align capital and terms.

    Pricing, payment, working capital and transfer of title are considered together. Trade finance is coordinated with suitable providers, subject to their review and agreed terms.

    Pricing basisPaymentTrade financeTitle
  4. Step 04

    Coordinate the movement.

    Loading, freight, inspection, cargo documents and discharge are arranged with specialist shipping and logistics partners.

    FreightInspectionCargo documentsDischarge
