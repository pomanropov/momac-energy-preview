Principal or broker. The role is agreed first.

Principal trading

As a principal, Momac buys the product, takes title and resells it. Roles, transfer of title and delivery obligations are set out in the agreements for each transaction.

Brokerage

As a broker, Momac brings a supplier and a buyer together and does not take title. Each party knows from the outset who holds title and who carries delivery obligations.
