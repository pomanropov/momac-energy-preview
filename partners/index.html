[04] Partners

Capital, counterparties and logistics.

A trade needs funding, a commercial match and a way to move the cargo. These are the three relationships Momac builds.

Capital

Capital partners are trade-finance institutions, lenders and capital partners. Funding requirements are set out at the start of each structured transaction. Any terms are discussed in personal conversation only.

Buyers and suppliers

Available supply is matched with a defined buying requirement. Specification, volume, terms and delivery are agreed, with a clear role for each party.

Logistics

Freight, inspection and delivery partners are chosen for what the cargo, the route and the agreed obligations call for.
